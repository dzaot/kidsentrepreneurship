## FAQ

### Plans and billing

**Is there a free trial of the family plan?**
The free plan is the trial: no card, no time limit. Upgrade when you're ready.

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes. Cancel from Settings in two taps. You keep the family plan until the end of the period you paid for, then you move to the free plan. Your kid's shop and progress stay.

> Nota: esto tiene que ser verdad en el producto cuando se lance el cobro. Nada de "llamar para cancelar".

**Does it cost anything to start selling?**
No. Digital products cost nothing to make and there's no inventory. When a buyer pays, your payment provider (Stripe, PayPal or Square) takes a small fee, usually around 3% plus a few cents. The price step shows your kid exactly what they keep.

**Do you take a cut of my kid's sales?**
No. Buyers pay through your own payment link, straight to you. We never touch the money.

**What if I have more than 4 kids?**
Write to us. We'll sort it out.

**Do you offer school or classroom plans?**
Not yet. It's on our roadmap. Let us know if your school is interested.

### Privacy and safety

**What do you collect about my kid?**
Only a nickname you choose, an avatar, an age group (8 to 10 or 11 to 14), lesson progress, their private journal answers, and the shop they build. Never a real name, photo, birthday, school, location, voice or contacts.

**Is anything about my kid public?**
Only the shop page, and only after you approve it. It shows the made-up shop name, the mascot, the product and the price. Never your kid's nickname, your email or account details. Shops are hidden from search engines and shared by link.

**Can my kid talk to strangers on Venturoo?**
No. There's no chat between users, no public profiles, no comments and no leaderboards. Buyers never contact your kid. Any messages come to you.

**Do you show ads or sell data?**
Never, on any plan. No ads, no third-party tracking pixels, no data sales. We use one cookie to keep you logged in.

**How do I delete our data?**
From Settings, any time. It permanently deletes your account, every kid profile, shop, file and sale record, right away. Live shops go offline immediately.

**Which law do you follow?**
We design for COPPA in the US, and for children's privacy rules in Canada and Australia. The account is always a parent's or guardian's, and kids use it under your supervision.

> Nota: no decir "COPPA compliant" ni "certified" hasta tener consentimiento verificable y, idealmente, un programa Safe Harbor (doc 03). "We design for COPPA" es la frase segura mientras tanto.

### AI and Guroo

**What is Guroo?**
Guroo is our kangaroo coach. In every lesson, she guides your kid. On the family plan, she can also chat as an AI helper for ideas, names, word lists, descriptions and image prompts to try together with you.

**Is the AI on by default?**
No. Guroo's chat is off until you turn it on for each kid, and lessons work fine without it.

**Where do my kid's messages go?**
Guroo runs on Claude by Anthropic. When it's on, the messages your kid types, plus their shop details and age group, are sent to Anthropic to write a reply. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models. Anything that looks like personal info (emails, phone numbers, addresses, links) is blocked before sending.

**Can I see what my kid and Guroo talk about?**
Yes. You can read and delete every chat from your parent zone. Your kid sees a note that says so.

**What won't Guroo do?**
Ask for personal info, pretend to be a person, talk about anything outside business, making and learning, give money, legal or medical advice, or suggest famous characters. If your kid seems upset or mentions anything unsafe, Guroo tells them to talk to you or another trusted adult.

**Is there a limit?**
Up to 40 messages a day per kid. It keeps sessions short and focused.

**Will my kid just let AI do everything?**
That's exactly what we teach against. The rule is "AI is your helper, you're the boss": kids write the prompt, choose, edit, add their own twist, and say when AI helped in the product description.

**Can Guroo make images?**
Not yet. AI image tools have their own age rules, so the lessons have kids write the prompt with Guroo and then try it with you, on your account.
