# App Store, Play Store and social copy

> **Nota para el fundador:** el listing de tiendas es para más adelante (la app empaquetada con Capacitor está en la fase 2 del roadmap). Todo es sin rostro y seguro para niños: ningún niño aparece con cara, voz o nombre, y los posts hablan a padres. **Description:**

Venturoo turns 5 minutes a day into your kid's first real business.

Kids 8 to 14 learn real business skills (customers, price, profit) in short, game-like lessons with Guroo, a wise kangaroo coach. Each lesson builds a piece of their own shop. In about 30 days, they have a real digital product, a real shop page, and a shot at their first online sale.

You own the account. You approve everything. Your kid's identity stays private the whole way.

HOW IT WORKS
• Learn: 5-minute lessons, quizzes and real-world missions
• Make: one digital product, like coloring pages, a sticker pack, wallpapers, a puzzle pack or a mini comic
• Open shop: a secret shop name, a mascot, a tagline and a price, all built inside the lessons
• First sale: you share the link with people you trust, buyers pay through your own payment link and get an instant download

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No faces, no real names. Kids sell as a made-up brand with a mascot
• Nothing goes public until a parent approves it. Unpublish anytime
• The money goes to your own payment link. We never touch it
• Nothing social between kids: no chat, no public profiles, no leaderboards
• A personal-info filter blocks emails, phone numbers, addresses and links
• No ads, no tracking pixels, no data sales. Delete everything anytime

GUROO, A KID-SAFE AI COACH
Guroo helps with ideas, names, word lists and descriptions. She's off until you turn her on, sticks to business and creativity, never asks for personal info, and you can read every chat. The rule we teach: AI is your helper, you're the boss.

PARENTS LEARN TOO
Every lesson has a Parent Corner: the grown-up version of the idea and one question to talk about together.

LEVEL 2
After the Launch Path, kids unlock Merch Makers (print-on-demand, the kid-friendly version of dropshipping) and Faceless YouTube Studio (helpful videos with AI as a helper, on a parent's channel).

Accounts are for parents and guardians 18+. Sales are never guaranteed. The skills, the pride and the habit are.

## 6. Do / don't for social

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Show hands, desks, paper, UI, Guroo | Show kid faces, even blurred, or school uniforms |
| Use real UI screenshots from the app | Mock up features we don't have (leaderboards, chat) |
| Say "pilot" | Say "thousands of families" or "#1" |
| Talk to parents | Ask kids to follow, comment or DM |
| Use made-up example shops (Ocean Doodle Co.) | Show a real kid's shop without written parent permission, and even then never their nickname |
